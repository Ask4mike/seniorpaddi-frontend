Chapter 1Welcome, and how to use this guide

This guide explains the SeniorPadi website from the ground up. By the end you will know how to run it on your own computer, find your way around its folders, and make changes with confidence.

What SeniorPadi is

SeniorPadi is a free website for people aged 60 and over in Ibadan, Nigeria. It helps them find community events near them (exercise groups, church programmes, skills classes, health talks and social gatherings), sign up to attend or volunteer, and, once staff have checked their ID, meet and chat with other seniors.

The problem it solves is loneliness and not knowing what is on nearby. Many of the people using it have simple phones, slow internet and sometimes a relative helping them. So the site is built to be calm, readable and forgiving: big text, big buttons, plain words, and it works in English and Yorùbá.

There are two kinds of people who use it:

  • Seniors sign up with a phone number, browse events, register, get a confirmation text and a reminder, and can message other verified seniors.
  • Staff (people at the NGO that runs the site) add organizations and events, mark who came, check IDs and handle reports. In the code, staff are called admin.
Important

Everything in this guide was checked against the real project files on the main branch. Every code snippet you see is copied straight out of the project by the program that builds this PDF, with its file name and line numbers. If a snippet ever stops matching, the build refuses to finish, so the guide cannot quietly go out of date.

Who this guide is for

You, if you are smart and curious but new to some or all of these: JavaScript on a server, Node.js, Express, EJS templates, databases and Tailwind CSS. You do not need any of them before you start. Every technical word is explained the first time it appears, and there is a glossary at the back (chapter 12).
